The Calendar timeline now covers the whole day: scroll sideways to see midnight to 6 AM. It opens at 6 AM, or earlier when the day has an earlier event, and always keeps the current time in view on today. Early events are regular blocks instead of a "Before 6 AM" row.

Scrolling the day ribbon is smoother: it no longer redraws the page or reformats dates as days pass the centre, and its edge fades no longer re-render the strip each frame.
