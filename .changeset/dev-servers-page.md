Add a Dev Servers page to Home: every local server you started (Next.js, Vite, Django, Rails, Postgres and more) with its port and project folder. Open one in the browser, or stop it after a confirmation. Apps, system services and coding-agent helpers are left out. It is also a command: search "Dev Servers" or "ports", or give it a shortcut in Settings → Shortcuts.

Home and its pages are wider (600 pt) so the dashboard tiles have more room; the search panel keeps its width.
