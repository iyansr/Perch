Add a Calendar page to Home: a scrollable ribbon of days, the selected day as a timeline, and the event's details with Join for Zoom, Meet and Teams. Click the month to jump to any month of any year; the arrow keys step by day or week. It is also a command: search "Calendar" or "schedule", or give it a shortcut in Settings → Shortcuts. Needs Calendar access.

Settings → General → 24-hour time shows calendar times as 20:00 instead of 8:00 PM; it starts on your region's clock.
