# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Perch to be a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful. Welcome different experiences and perspectives.
- Give and accept constructive feedback. Focus discussion on ideas and behavior.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and respect others' privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces and when someone represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer using a contact method listed at [iyansr's website](https://links.iyansr.id). Do not post sensitive information in a public issue. Include enough context for the maintainer to assess the concern, and share private information only through a channel you consider appropriate.

The maintainer will review reports and may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation as appropriate. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical.
